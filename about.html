About Gene Tents Ltd

Gene Tents Ltd is a Ugandan tent manufacturer and hire company based in Namanve, Kampala. We make wedding tents, camping tents, car shades and pickup covers, and we hire out tents for events of every size.

We believe good shelter should be strong, neat and affordable. That is why we build in our own workshop and fit every product ourselves, from first measurement to final stitch.

Whether you are hosting a wedding for 800 guests or covering one pickup, you get the same careful work and honest advice.

How we work

1. Tell us what you need

Call, message or visit with your size, date and budget.

2. We quote and measure

You get a clear price. For custom jobs we measure on site.

3. We build or deliver

We make your order, or set up your hired tent on the day.

4. We take care of the rest

Hired tents are collected after your event.
