How to choose the right tents in Uganda

By Gene Tents Ltd · Namanve, Kampala

The right tent depends on how many people it must hold, what it will be used for, and how long it must last. Use these steps before you pay.

1. Decide the purpose

A wedding marquee, a camping tent and a car shade are built very differently. Be clear about the main use so the supplier can recommend the right frame, fabric and shape.

2. Work out the size

As a guide, allow about 1.5 square metres for each seated guest at round tables. Allow more for a dance floor, stage, buffet or bar. A 10 × 20 metre tent seats around 130 guests with tables. Measure your compound or venue too, because the tent must fit with space for walkways.

3. Check the fabric

  • Choose waterproof fabric for the rainy seasons (March to May and September to November).
  • Choose UV-resistant fabric so the colour does not fade and tear in strong sun.
  • Look for strong stitching and reinforced corners and seams.

4. Check the frame

Strong steel or galvanised frames last longer and hold better in wind. Ask how the tent is fixed to the ground, with pegs, base plates or weights.

5. Think about weather and ventilation

A tent with side walls, windows or removable sides keeps rain out and lets air move on hot days. For events, ask about gutters between joined tents so rain does not leak where they meet.

6. Compare buying and hiring

Hire if you need a tent only occasionally. Buy if you will use it regularly. Ask whether set-up, take-down, chairs and tables are included.

7. Ask about after-sales support

Good tents sometimes need repairs. Choose a supplier in Kampala who can fix tears, replace parts and answer questions after the sale.
